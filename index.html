Who cleans the mocap?

The system does the cleanup. You steer it.

Every capture runs the full cleanup below without being asked. Your job is to give it good footage and the two numbers a camera cannot measure, then choose how the result lands on your rig. Re-baking with different options takes seconds and never re-runs the capture.
